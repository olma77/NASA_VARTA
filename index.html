      About

      FireWatch combines two decades of satellite fire detections (MODIS since 2000, VIIRS since 2012) into one comparable calendar for each country or any area you select. It highlights unusual periods and estimates the chance of high fire activity in the next days. It is a statistical early-warning aid for analysts, not an observation of any single fire. Data: NASA FIRMS. Weather: Open-Meteo.
